## Notes

- `/store` is seller-only. Tawk is hidden here. WhatsApp product widget can hide Tawk on selected product pages.
- Some policy URLs are aliases (`/privacy` and `/privacy-policy`).
- Product pages exist as both `/product/[slug]` and `/products/[slug]`.
